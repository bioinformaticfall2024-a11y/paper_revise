FULL DRAFT RESPONSE TO REVIEWER 3 — COMMENT 19
We thank the reviewer for this valuable suggestion and agree that extending the cold-target evaluation to PVgraphDTA would provide additional insight into the generalization capability of its multimodal structural representation, particularly when predicting affinities for previously unseen protein targets.

In the present study, we introduced two complementary approaches to drug–target affinity prediction, FGgraphDTA and PVgraphDTA, which address different aspects of molecular and protein representation. FGgraphDTA investigates the contribution of functional-group enrichment to ligand and protein graph representations, whereas PVgraphDTA integrates graph-based representations with contact-map-derived visual features and contrastive learning. Both models were evaluated on the DAVIS and KIBA benchmark datasets under the reported experimental protocols.

To further investigate generalization beyond random interaction splits, we conducted an additional protein-disjoint cold-target evaluation on the DAVIS dataset using FGgraphDTA and the DGraphDTA baseline. In this experiment, 58 proteins, representing approximately 20% of the 287 usable protein targets, were held out entirely from training, resulting in 3,944 test interactions. No interactions involving the held-out proteins were included in the training set.

Under this evaluation protocol, FGgraphDTA achieved an MSE of 0.4252, a concordance index of 0.8106, and a Spearman correlation of 0.7020, compared with an MSE of 0.4460, a concordance index of 0.8020, and a Spearman correlation of 0.6884 for DGraphDTA. These results provide evidence that the functional-group-enriched graph representation improved performance relative to the graph-based baseline on the held-out DAVIS proteins in this particular split. The experiment provides an additional, protein-disjoint evaluation of FGgraphDTA beyond the conventional random interaction split; however, a single split does not establish generalization across all possible held-out protein cohorts.

Regarding the extension of this evaluation to PVgraphDTA, we acknowledge that such an experiment would be valuable. However, our available computational resources were limited, and conducting an additional cold-target evaluation of the complete PVgraphDTA framework was not feasible within the computational budget of the present revision. Although the ResNet-101 visual backbone remains frozen during DTA training and the autoencoder representations are precomputed, the multimodal framework requires structural-image processing and integration of additional feature streams. Extending the evaluation would also require preparing and checking the structural representations and retraining and evaluating the PVgraphDTA predictor under the protein-disjoint protocol.

Given these constraints, we prioritized the paired FGgraphDTA–DGraphDTA cold-target comparison, which permits an assessment of the effect of functional-group enrichment under a consistent unseen-target split. Together with the DAVIS and KIBA benchmark evaluations and the component-wise ablation analyses, this constitutes the experimental scope of the present study.

We emphasize that the FGgraphDTA cold-target results should not be interpreted as direct evidence of PVgraphDTA's performance on unseen proteins. The main PVgraphDTA results describe its behavior under the reported DAVIS and KIBA benchmark protocols, while its performance under protein-disjoint conditions remains untested. In particular, the present results cannot determine whether adding the contact-map visual branch improves or harms predictions for unseen protein targets.

We have clarified this distinction in the Discussion and explicitly identified protein-disjoint evaluation of PVgraphDTA as an important direction for future work. We hope that the additional cold-target comparison for FGgraphDTA, alongside the benchmark and ablation analyses, provides a meaningful assessment of the investigated representations within the computational scope of the present study, while acknowledging the remaining generalization question for PVgraphDTA.
