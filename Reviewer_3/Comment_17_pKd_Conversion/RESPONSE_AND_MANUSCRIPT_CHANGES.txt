RESPONSE TO REVIEWER — COMMENT 17

We thank the reviewer for highlighting the need to clarify the DAVIS affinity transformation. Our affinity representation follows the original DGraphDTA methodology (Jiang et al., 2020), in which dissociation constants reported in nanomolar are converted to the pKd scale using

    pKd = -log10(Kd_nM / 10^9) = 9 - log10(Kd_nM).

Thus, the negative logarithm was not applied directly to the raw nanomolar values.

Regarding the truncation threshold, we examined the affinity labels in our processed DAVIS dataset. The stored pKd values range from 5.0000 to 10.6198, with no values below 5. The lower boundary of pKd = 5 corresponds to a dissociation constant of 10,000 nM (10 µM), consistent with the benchmark representation of weak or undetected binding measurements. The processed dataset already contained affinity labels on this scale, and no additional truncation was applied during model training.

We agree that the previous manuscript did not specify the unit conversion and affinity-label boundary sufficiently clearly. We have therefore revised the DAVIS dataset description to provide the explicit conversion formula and clarify the lower boundary present in the processed affinity labels. These clarifications do not change the affinity values used for training or the previously reported experimental results.
