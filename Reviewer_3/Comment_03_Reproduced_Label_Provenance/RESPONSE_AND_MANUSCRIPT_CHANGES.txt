A. REVIEWER RESPONSE (English — use after manuscript edits)
====================================================================
We thank the reviewer for highlighting the ambiguity of the label "reproduced" and the need to distinguish literature-reported values from an independently evaluated matched baseline. Although we ran the publicly available DGraphDTA implementation during our work, the previously displayed baseline rows were not accompanied by the prediction files, exact split indices, random seeds, and environment records required to establish that those table entries represented matched reruns under the evaluation protocol of the proposed models. We therefore elected not to present those entries as reproduced or matched-baseline results in the revised manuscript.

To remove this ambiguity, we have replaced the DGraphDTA comparison rows with the values reported in the original DGraphDTA publication and have explicitly labeled them "literature-reported" in both benchmark tables. For DAVIS, the published DGraphDTA GCN&GCN results are CI = 0.904, MSE = 0.202, and Pearson = 0.867; for KIBA, they are CI = 0.904, MSE = 0.126, and Pearson = 0.903. Thus, the DAVIS numerical entries remain unchanged, whereas the previous KIBA numerical entries (CI = 0.891, MSE = 0.147, Pearson = 0.903) have been replaced with the original published values. The previous KIBA results are not retained as a separate reproduced row in the revised comparison table.

We have also clarified in the table notes and the experimental comparison text that the DGraphDTA entries are literature-reported reference values, not results obtained under identical train/validation/test partitions to those of our proposed models. Accordingly, cross-study numerical differences are described as descriptive reference comparisons rather than controlled head-to-head performance differences. We do not infer from the prior DAVIS agreement or the prior KIBA discrepancy alone whether any particular run was or was not independently executed; the revision instead makes the provenance and limits of the displayed results explicit. Statements and figures affected by the corrected KIBA DGraphDTA values have been updated as detailed in our response to Comment 1.
