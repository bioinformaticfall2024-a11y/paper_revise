RESPONSE TO REVIEWER

We thank the reviewer for highlighting these implementation details. The contact-probability threshold for constructing protein-graph edges and the autoencoder mask threshold are independent parameters used in separate stages. In our protein-graph implementation, residue pairs are connected when their predicted contact probability is at least 0.5; the separate threshold of 0.05 is used for the autoencoder reconstruction mask and does not determine graph edges.

Regarding the cold-target experiment, the reported cold-target results concern DGraphDTA and FGgraphDTA, not the autoencoder-based PVgraphDTA model. In addition, the autoencoder was not trained using contact maps from held-out test proteins. Accordingly, held-out test-protein contact maps did not contribute to learning its parameters, and there was no test-target information leakage through autoencoder training. We appreciate the opportunity to clarify the distinction between these components and the evaluation protocol.
