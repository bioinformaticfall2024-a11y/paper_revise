RESPONSE TO REVIEWER 3, COMMENT 14 (paste after making manuscript changes)

We thank the reviewer for emphasizing the importance of repeated experimental evaluations and statistical uncertainty quantification. We agree that repeating training over different random seeds and testing several independent protein-disjoint splits would permit a more complete assessment of stability and generalization.

The requested five training runs for each principal model–dataset configuration, together with repeated cold-target training, exceed the computational resources currently available to us. In particular, the PVgraphDTA workflow includes additional structural preprocessing and visual feature extraction. We therefore could not complete the requested repeated-run experiments within this revision. We recognize that computational limitations do not eliminate the resulting statistical uncertainty.

The main DAVIS and KIBA results are single-run point estimates; they should not be interpreted as estimates of run-to-run reproducibility. In the existing DAVIS cold-target evaluation, FGgraphDTA achieved MSE = 0.4252, CI = 0.8106 and Spearman rho = 0.7020 on 58 held-out proteins and 3,944 test interactions. The previously reported two-sided paired per-protein MSE test yielded t(57) = 2.13, p = 0.038. This unadjusted test is limited to one selected protein-disjoint split and does not establish robustness to changes of training seed or held-out proteins.

We have not reported a bootstrap interval because the underlying paired protein-level observations or test predictions needed for valid nonparametric resampling have not been made available for this revision; the summary metrics and t-statistic alone do not identify the empirical bootstrap distribution. We have revised the Experimental Setup, cold-target Results, Discussion, and Conclusion to state these limitations clearly and to avoid claims of statistically established improvements across seeds or independent partitions. We recognize that repeated-seed and repeated-split validation remains outstanding and thank the reviewer for identifying this limitation.
