RESPONSE TO REVIEWER — COMMENT 15

We thank the reviewer for allowing us to clarify the DAVIS populations. The values of 442 proteins and 30,056 pairs describe the complete public DAVIS matrix (68 × 442). The cold-target analysis used a separately defined structure-eligible cohort of 287 proteins and 19,516 pairs (68 × 287). Of these, 58 complete targets and 3,944 pairs (68 × 58) were held out for testing, leaving 229 targets and 15,572 pairs (68 × 229) for development. These counts are internally consistent and reflect the exclusion of complete targets rather than missing drug–target interactions within the retained cohort. - A matched test cohort avoids comparing the two models on different test samples; it does NOT prove that filtering cannot change relative performance.
- Do not claim a 183-target training / 46-target validation split unless that exact split was used in the original experiment.
- The reviewer asks whether filtering occurred before splitting, and for rejection counts and a manifest. These remain to be verified rather than invented.
