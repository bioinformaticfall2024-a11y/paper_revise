PAPER TEXT
On DAVIS, PVgraphDTA underperformed both FGgraphDTA and the graph-only reference across the reported metrics. The visual branch was a net negative contributor under the present DAVIS configuration; AE gating and InfoNCE recovered part, but not all, of that loss. The cause cannot be isolated from these data alone and may involve contact-map quality, visual-encoder capacity, optimization, or dataset characteristics.
