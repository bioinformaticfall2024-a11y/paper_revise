A. RESPONSE TO REVIEWER — ENGLISH, COPY-READY AFTER APPLYING EDITS
======================================================================
We thank the reviewer for identifying that our previous wording understated the negative DAVIS result. We agree that it was insufficient to state only that PVgraphDTA performed worse than FGgraphDTA. In the revised DAVIS Results, we explicitly report that PVgraphDTA (MSE = 0.207, CI = 0.875, Pearson = 0.849) also has numerically less favorable values on all three metrics than the published DGraphDTA reference (MSE = 0.202, CI = 0.904, Pearson = 0.867). In line with our response to Comment 3, the DGraphDTA values are now identified as literature-reported and this particular comparison is described as descriptive rather than a matched evaluation under identical data partitions.

We have also revised the PVgraphDTA ablation interpretation to state the negative contribution of the visual branch directly. On DAVIS, adding the ResNet image branch to the graph-only scaffold increased MSE from 0.2018 to 0.2216 and decreased CI from 0.9034 to 0.8648. Autoencoder gating and InfoNCE progressively improved the multimodal configurations (MSE = 0.2148 and 0.2070, respectively), but the full PVgraphDTA configuration still had higher MSE (0.2070 versus 0.2018) and lower CI (0.8750 versus 0.9034) than the graph-only control. Thus, the added visual pathway had a net negative effect on the reported DAVIS ablation metrics in the evaluated configuration, despite partial recovery by the additional components.

Finally, we revised the Discussion to acknowledge this limitation explicitly and removed the unsupported attribution of the DAVIS decrease solely to dataset size. Dataset size, contact-map quality, model complexity and optimization may warrant future investigation, but the current ablation does not establish any of them as the cause. Our conclusion is restricted to the observed DAVIS configuration rather than claiming that visual protein information is generally unhelpful.
