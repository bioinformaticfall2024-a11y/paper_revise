============================================================
A. RESPONSE TO REVIEWER
============================================================

We thank the reviewer for this constructive suggestion. Following the reviewer’s recommendation, we performed an additional PVgraphDTA experiment using AlphaFold2-derived protein structural information instead of the PconsC4-derived contact maps used in the primary experiments.

The AlphaFold2-based PVgraphDTA experiment achieved an MSE of 0.2525, a CI of 0.8654, a Pearson correlation of 0.8124, and a Spearman correlation of 0.6547 on the held-out test set.

The additional experiment indicates that replacing the original structural input with AlphaFold2-derived structural information does not by itself resolve the weaker performance of PVgraphDTA on DAVIS. We therefore revised the manuscript to avoid attributing this behavior solely to dataset size. Instead, the revised Discussion considers structural-input quality together with model complexity and the multimodal visual/fusion architecture as possible contributing factors.

The AlphaFold2 experiment and the corresponding discussion have been added to the revised manuscript.
