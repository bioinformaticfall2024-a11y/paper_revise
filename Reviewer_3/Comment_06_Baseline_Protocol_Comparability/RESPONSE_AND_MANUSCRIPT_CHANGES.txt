A. RESPONSE TO REVIEWER — COPY-READY ENGLISH
=====================================================================
We thank the reviewer for highlighting the limitations of comparing literature-reported baseline results with the results of our proposed models under different evaluation protocols. We agree that our previous presentation did not sufficiently distinguish the sources of these results or their respective evaluation procedures. In particular, the label "reproduced" was inconsistent with the statement in the Experimental Protocol that baseline values were taken from previous publications.

As detailed in our response to Comment 3, we have removed the "reproduced" label from the DGraphDTA comparison rows and replaced those entries with the values reported in the original DGraphDTA publication. We now identify them explicitly as literature-reported values rather than independently reproduced baselines evaluated under the same data partitions as the proposed methods. We have also revised the comparison-table notes and the Experimental Protocol section to distinguish results generated in the present study from published reference values.

We acknowledge that FGgraphDTA and PVgraphDTA were evaluated using a single 80/10/10 train/validation/test split, whereas the published comparison methods were evaluated under their respective protocols, including cross-validation procedures in several cited studies. Therefore, numerical differences between these results cannot be interpreted as controlled, matched performance improvements or used to establish the statistical reliability of relative model performance.

We have removed the statement that FGgraphDTA achieves a 23.8% improvement in DAVIS MSE over DGraphDTA. Although the numerical MSE values shown in the two studies differ, the evaluation conditions are not sufficiently matched to attribute the difference to the proposed architecture. The revised Results and Discussion describe cross-publication numerical differences as descriptive reference comparisons and identify the lack of a matched, repeated-run comparison as a limitation.

We also acknowledge that the previously requested rerunning of all literature baselines under our exact partitions has not been completed. We therefore do not present the revised tables as satisfying that experimental request. A controlled assessment of relative performance would require evaluation of the baselines and proposed models on identical partitions, repeated runs, and appropriate uncertainty reporting.

Finally, we thank the reviewer for confirming that the published DeepDTA CNN–CNN KIBA values (CI = 0.863 and MSE = 0.194) are correctly reported; these values remain unchanged. The separate error in the DGraphDTA KIBA row has been corrected to its original published values, as described in our responses to Comments 1 and 3.
